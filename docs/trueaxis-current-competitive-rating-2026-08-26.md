## What the Rating Means

TrueAxis HQ is now a **high-depth, trust-first service operations platform**. Its clearest differentiators are client-safe proof-of-work continuity, scoped approval and document sharing, owner clarity controls, recoverable public flows, connected field-to-finance operations, and testable workflow security boundaries.

It is **not yet rated 5/5** because the remaining differences are material: real SMTP and Stripe delivery verification, authenticated mobile and owner/client usability evidence, live accounting/communications/calendar integrations, staff permissions, and GPS/route/technician dispatch depth. Those require provider access, real accounts, or independent user evidence; code alone should not erase that distinction.
