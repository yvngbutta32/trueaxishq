# Service Catalog and Scheduling Research

Official Housecall Pro booking guidance documents service-type preferences including whether a service is shown in online booking, a service duration, and job-versus-estimate behavior. It also describes booking hours and flexible or fixed start-time scheduling.[1]

Official HoneyBook scheduler guidance documents reusable session types with duration, availability, buffers, minimum notice, and increments.[2]

Official Jobber booking guidance documents request, assessment, and direct job booking forms based on availability, including different booking flows for review-before-scheduling versus direct scheduling.[3]

TrueAxis HQ’s in-progress structured catalog is intentionally narrower: backward-compatible active service definitions, duration persistence, optional owner price guidance, and later safe buffers. It does not yet claim custom calendar synchronization, routing, automatic payments, provider delivery, or the broader booking modes described by these products.
