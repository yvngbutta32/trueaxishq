# Competitor Scorecard Refresh — Source Notes

**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Purpose:** External evidence notes for a current, evidence-led TrueAxis HQ comparison. Official feature claims establish availability, not quality or equivalence. Review-site observations are attributable themes and individual experiences, not representative measurements or findings about all users.

| Product | Official capability observations | Attributable friction themes to assess carefully |
|---|---|---|
| HoneyBook | The official site describes lead capture, scheduling, proposals, contracts, invoices/payments, CRM/client portal, tasks, automations, AI, and integrations. | A Trustpilot review summary and individual recent reviews mention occasional performance issues, learning/setup effort, and some pricing or workflow concerns; these are not universal findings. |
| Bonsai | The official site describes client management, tasks, Gantt/project management, time tracking, budgets, client portal, reporting, resource planning, invoicing, payments, expenses, forecasting, and integrations. | The collected review sources describe payment delays, support variability, and scaling/team-depth limitations as user-reported themes, not population measures. |
| Jobber | The official features page describes quoting, invoices/payments, online booking, requests, work orders, recurring visits, scheduling/dispatch, field documentation, client hub, staff controls, GPS/routing, notifications, and marketing features. | Capterra reviews include reports of reporting or customization friction, integration mismatch, and web/mobile experience differences; the page presents a mixture of incentivized and non-incentivized reviews. |
| Housecall Pro | The official features page describes invoicing/payments, scheduling, pipeline, estimates, dispatch, GPS, job costing, recurring plans, field mobile use, customer portal, communications, marketing, and integrations. | Capterra reviews include individual reports of customization/reporting limits, intermittent stability or support issues, and connectivity friction. Those reviews are not representative evidence. |
| Dubsado | The official feature overview describes lead forms, booking, proposals, contracts/templates, client access, invoices/payments, reminders, recurring billing, task actions, dashboards, and business metrics. | G2’s user-insight summary and individual reviews mention learning curve, setup complexity, stale-page/bug reports, and client-portal/project-management usability concerns. Review attribution and incentive labels vary. |
| ServiceTitan | The official features page describes enterprise contractor CRM/sales, construction management, service agreements, scheduling/dispatch, field apps/forms/timekeeping, accounting/AP, inventory, customer portal, payments, integrations, API, and GPS/technician tracking. | Public review themes from the collected sources include implementation complexity, support responsiveness, and stability concerns. They remain non-representative. |

## Evidence limits

These sources do not establish TrueAxis HQ’s production reliability, real customer outcomes, legal enforceability, provider delivery, or market rank. The collected competitor pages also contain vendor marketing claims that are not adopted as factual performance benchmarks in the resulting scorecard.
