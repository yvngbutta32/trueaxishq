# TrueAxis HQ Operations Expansion Evidence

**Prepared:** August 25, 2026  
**Scope:** Evidence for the team operations, capacity, dispatch, and integration-readiness expansion initiated from the competitor scorecard.

## What Was Added

TrueAxis HQ now contains a coherent operations layer rather than isolated feature claims. An owner can maintain an internal team roster, set weekly planning capacity, assign accountable people to jobs, review planned workload, schedule service visits, move a visit through internal dispatch states, and maintain a provider-readiness catalog. The new model builds directly on the existing job, client, booking, proof, time, expense, invoice, and portal foundations.

| Expansion area | Implemented, evidence-backed capability | Explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Team operations | Owner-scoped team roster with operational role, contact reference, active state, color, weekly capacity, archival/reactivation, and capacity summaries. | Roster records are **not** staff accounts; they do not grant a login or cross-workspace access. |
| Resource planning | Unique owner/job/member assignments, assignment role/state, planned minutes, owner-only status updates, and job timeline activity records. | Capacity is internal planning information, not payroll, attendance, or time-clock enforcement. |
| Dispatch | Job-assignment prerequisite; service visit records; internal site/dispatch note; schedule validation; explicit overlap acknowledgement; manual states through scheduled, en route, in progress, completed, and cancelled. | No GPS, live technician location, route optimization, automated ETA, vehicle tracking, or automatic client notification is claimed. |
| Client coordination | Service work stays connected to the existing job activity and curated client update flow, so internal logistics remain private. | Assignments and dispatch notes are not exposed to the client portal by default. |
| Integration ecosystem | Nine-provider catalog across calendar, accounting, communications, automation, and payments; per-owner readiness metadata; Google authorization inferred from the secure token record. | No provider is marked connected from a UI input; live third-party connectivity needs account authorization and controlled verification. |

## Integrity and Security Controls

The database now includes owner-scoped `teamMembers`, `jobAssignments`, `serviceVisits`, and `integrationConnections` tables. Job assignment writes first verify both the target job and roster member under the authenticated owner, store the owner ID on the assignment, enforce a per-owner job/member uniqueness constraint, and write an operational activity record. Service visits require an active, owner-scoped job assignment; scheduling conflicts are blocked unless the owner deliberately acknowledges the exception. All updates and deletes use owner predicates.

The integration catalog has a separate trust boundary. Its preparation flow can save only a `needs_configuration` state. It cannot accept `connected` from the client. Google Calendar state is derived from the existing secure calendar-token record; removal deletes that local authorization record under the authenticated owner scope. Credentials and provider tokens are not stored in the readiness table.

## Competitive Effect

The release directly reduces the documented gaps against agency and field-service platforms: TrueAxis HQ now has an owner-safe team/capacity model, an accountable dispatch workflow, and an integration-ready ecosystem model. It should **not** be described as having the mature live-routing, broad connector marketplace, multi-user permissions, or technician-mobile ecosystem that incumbents have validated in the market. The new foundation narrows the structural gap and creates a secure path to close it with provider authorization, staff-role design, and real-workflow evidence.

## Remaining Gates

> **The remaining gaps are not unimplemented autonomous code defects.** They require actual external accounts, provider tokens, real users, or independent assurance.

| Gate | Why it remains external |
|---|---|
| Google Calendar, email, payments, accounting, communication, and automation connections | Each needs provider authorization, credentials, scopes, callback/webhook verification, and controlled delivery tests. |
| Stripe payment flow | Requires claimed sandbox, signing secret, and a verified end-to-end checkout/webhook scenario. |
| Authenticated owner mobile review | The available test browser has no owner session, so Team, Dispatch, Integration Hub, and Field Mode need a controlled owner-session 375px walkthrough. |
| Staff authentication and permissions | The product intentionally uses an owner-managed roster first; staff accounts require explicit membership, role policy, and authorization design rather than an unsafe email-based shortcut. |
| Live routing / technician location | Requires a chosen mapping/location provider, consent, retention policy, and provider verification. |
| Independent security and legal assurance | Requires qualified third-party review and cannot be substituted by automated tests. |
