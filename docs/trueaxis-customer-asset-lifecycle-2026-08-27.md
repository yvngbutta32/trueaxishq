# Customer-Asset Lifecycle — 2026-08-27

Owners can now deactivate or reactivate a private customer asset from Job Workspace. Deactivation retains the owner-scoped record for reference but removes it from new job-link choices; it does not delete the record, alter historical jobs, expose it to the client portal, or message a client.

The lifecycle mutation is constrained by the asset ID and authenticated owner ID. Validation passed with strict TypeScript, 86 Vitest files / 230 tests, a production build, and configured bundle budgets.
