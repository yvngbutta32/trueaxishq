# ServiceM8 Competitor Fact File (October 2026)

## Facts
* **Pricing Model & Tiers**: Published job-volume monthly plans with unlimited users on official pricing page (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/pricing).
  - Free: $0/mo (30 jobs/month)
  - Starter: $29/mo (50 jobs/month)
  - Growing: $79/mo (150 jobs/month)
  - Premium: $149/mo (500 jobs/month)
  - Premium Plus: $349/mo (1,500 jobs/month)
* **Contract & Implementation**: Month-to-month subscription with no setup fees, no lock-in contracts, and zero per-seat user fees (Source: https://support.servicem8.com/questions/general/how-does-servicem8-pricing-work).
* **AI Capabilities**: Native AI suite includes AI Quoting & Invoicing assistant, AI writing helper for client communications, and automated audio note transcription (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/ai-job-management-software-for-trade-contractors, https://www.chanautomation.com/blog/ai-in-job-management-software).
* **ServiceM8 Phone Agent (AI Receptionist)**: AI Virtual Receptionist add-on for ServiceM8 Phone that answers calls 24/7, collects caller information, and automatically creates job cards (Source: https://support.servicem8.com/help-center/servicem8-add-ons/servicem8-phone-agent/introducing-the-servicem8-phone-agent).
* **AI Pricing**: ServiceM8 Phone Agent starts at $49/mo (or $44/mo annually) + GST / usage add-on (Source: https://johnni.ai/servicem8-ai-receptionist/).
* **G2 Rating**: 3.8 out of 5 stars based on 15 verified reviews (Source: https://www.g2.com/products/servicem8/competitors/alternatives).
* **Capterra Rating**: 4.5 out of 5 stars based on ~311 verified reviews (Source: https://www.capterra.com/compare/110711-150053/ServiceM8-vs-ServiceTitan).
* **iOS App Store Rating**: ServiceM8 Field Service App holds ~4.6 out of 5 stars (Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/servicem8-field-service-app/id378062736); ServiceM8 Phone holds ~4.5 out of 5 stars (Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/servicem8-phone/id1525034898).
* **Google Play Rating**: iOS-first software with no full native Android app; companion ServiceM8 developer apps rate ~3.8 out of 5 stars (Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=ServiceM8&hl=en).
* **2026 Product & Pricing Changes**: Rollout of ServiceM8 Phone Agent AI virtual receptionist, Tap to Pay on iPhone integration, and AI Auto-Invoicing capabilities (Source: https://contractortoolstack.com/software/servicetitan/, https://support.servicem8.com/help-center/servicem8-add-ons/servicem8-phone-agent/introducing-the-servicem8-phone-agent).
* **#1 Complaint Theme**: Strict monthly job-volume caps forcing sudden subscription price jumps during busy seasons, combined with lack of a full-featured native Android mobile app (Source: https://myquoteiq.com/best-servicem8-alternative-for-handyman-businesses/, https://connecteam.com/reviews/servicem8/).
