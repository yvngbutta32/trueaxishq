# Jobber - Deep-Dive Research (October 2026)

## Facts

- **Jobber Client Hub Capabilities**: Client Hub is a 24/7 self-service customer portal where clients request appointments, approve quotes online with optional add-on selection and digital signatures, track tech arrival in real-time, view scheduled visits, pay invoices online via Jobber Payments/credit card/Apple Pay, print receipts, and refer friends via a built-in referral program. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/features/client-hub/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub/]
- **Jobber Copilot Capabilities & Limits**: Copilot acts as an AI business coach and data analyst that synthesizes historical account data (cash flow, team efficiency, performance) to offer operational advice, draft quote descriptions, and suggest growth strategies; it is included free for US/Canada accounts but relies strictly on historical Jobber data. [Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jobber-launches-copilot-the-first-of-several-ai-powered-products-aimed-at-making-home-service-business-ownership-simpler-than-ever-before-302264047.html / https://www.getjobber.com/features/ai/]
- **Jobber Receptionist AI Capabilities & Limits**: Receptionist AI provides 24/7 automated call and text handling, lead capture, and job booking directly into Jobber; priced as a $29/mo add-on for 30 conversations (+$0.79/conversation overage) or included free in the $499/mo Plus plan. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai/]
- **Quoting / Good-Better-Best System**: Jobber's quoting system allows contractors to present optional line items, upsell add-ons, and package choices (Good-Better-Best) directly on proposals; clients select items dynamically inside Client Hub, updating pricing before approving with digital signatures and deposits. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/optional-line-items-on-quotes/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quoting-on-the-grow-plan/]
- **Product Weakness - QuickBooks Sync Bugs**: Real user reviews cite frequent QuickBooks Online sync errors, duplicate invoice/payment records, and sync failures that require manual reconciliation. [Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/127994/Jobber/reviews/ / https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/jobber/]
- **Product Weakness - Native Inventory & Job Costing Gaps**: Jobber lacks native parts inventory management and deep job costing (vendor receipts, purchase orders), forcing reliance on third-party integrations or external tools. [Source: https://myquoteiq.com/jobbers-biggest-problem-exposed/ / https://contractortoolstack.com/software/jobber/]
- **Product Weakness - Mobile App Offline & Gated Features**: Mobile app lacks complete offline access for low-connectivity job sites, and key capabilities like 2-way SMS (Connect $139+/mo) and Open API (Plus $499/mo) are paywalled to higher tiers. [Source: https://checkthat.ai/brands/jobber/reviews / https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **Most-Requested Features**: Native warehouse/parts inventory management, deep job costing with purchase order tracking, full offline mobile syncing, and lower-tier 2-way SMS access. [Source: https://contractortoolstack.com/software/jobber/ / https://myquoteiq.com/jobbers-biggest-problem-exposed/]
- **Onboarding Time-to-Value**: Setup time is low, taking 1-3 days for solo operators and 1-2 weeks for small crews due to intuitive UX and self-guided templates. [Source: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/jobber/]
- **Support Quality Reputation**: Support is highly rated for rapid live chat response (<2-3 minutes average) and comprehensive Jobber Academy resources, though phone support is restricted on base tiers. [Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/127994/Jobber/reviews/]
- **2026 Product Launches**: Jobber Now 2026 introduced Projects management, multi-tech Crew Scheduling, Apple CarPlay & Android Auto field integration, updated Files and Media Library, Team @Mentions, automated Yelp lead sync, Sequifi payroll sync, and property merging. [Source: https://productupdates.getjobber.com/ / https://www.getjobber.com/events/jobber-now-2026/]
