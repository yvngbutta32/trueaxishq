# Kickserv Competitor Research (October 2026)

## Facts

* **Pricing Tiers & Terms**: Start tier is $60/mo (up to 5 users), Run tier is $119/mo (up to 10 users), and Scale tier is $199/mo (up to 20 users) billed monthly. Annual billing saves 20% (~$48/mo Start, ~$95/mo Run, ~$159/mo Scale). Free tier is retired in favor of a 30-day free trial. The Kickserv Kickback program offers an additional 5% discount when processing monthly payment volume minimums ($1k–$3k/mo). No annual lock-in required on monthly plans. Source: https://www.kickserv.com/pricing
* **Feature Paywall Breakdown**:
  - *Start ($60/mo, 5 users)*: Up to 5 users, embeddable contact form, automated email & text reminders, 20+ business reports, time tracking, expense tracking, QuickBooks Online integration, customer portal/invoicing.
  - *Run ($119/mo, 10 users)*: Up to 10 users, everything in Start plus dispatch mapping, GPS check-ins, custom data fields, custom reporting, optional QuickBooks Desktop integration (+$50/mo add-on).
  - *Scale ($199/mo, 20 users)*: Up to 20 users, everything in Run plus custom templates and review generation request tools.
  Source: https://www.kickserv.com/pricing
* **AI Capabilities**: No native AI voice receptionist, AI virtual assistant, or AI-powered workflow features. No dedicated AI add-ons available. Source: https://www.kickserv.com/pricing & https://www.kickserv.com/
* **Integrations & Payments**: Integrates natively with QuickBooks Online (included on Start+), QuickBooks Desktop (+$50/mo on Run+), Stripe, Mailchimp, Podium, Zapier, and Open API. Payment processing via Stripe supports credit cards and ACH; no native BNPL or proprietary instant payout system beyond standard Stripe capabilities. Source: https://www.kickserv.com/pricing
* **Reputation & Ratings**:
  - G2 Rating: 4.5 / 5 stars (181 reviews). Source: https://www.g2.com/products/kickserv/features
  - Capterra Rating: 4.4 / 5 stars (387 reviews). Source: https://www.capterra.com/compare/122541-147525/Kickserv-vs-Workiz
  - iOS App Store Rating: 3.8 / 5 stars (61 ratings). Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kickserv-field-service/id598686777
  - Google Play Rating: ~2.8 / 5 stars (~100+ ratings). Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kickserv.kickserv
* **Complaint Theme & Standout Strength**:
  - *#1 Complaint Theme*: Dated, unintuitive mobile app experience (especially on Android with frequent sync glitches and missing field features) and rigid per-user tier caps.
  - *Standout Strength*: Robust, deep QuickBooks integration (both Online and Desktop) combined with straightforward, affordable team-based seat bundles.
  Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/122541/Kickserv/reviews/ & https://www.getapp.com/operations-management-software/a/kickserv/
* **Target Segment**: Micro businesses and small growing crews (1–20 technicians/users) in trades like HVAC, plumbing, electrical, restoration, and cleaning. Source: https://www.kickserv.com/pricing
