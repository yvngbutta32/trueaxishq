# Jobber - Competitor Analysis (October 2026)

## Overview
Jobber is a leading B2B field-service-management (FSM) SaaS targeted at home service freelancers and small-to-medium field crews.

## Facts

- **Official Pricing Page**: Jobber pricing operates on tiered plans with base user caps and per-user add-on fees. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **Core Tier Pricing**: Core costs $49/mo (billed monthly) or $29/mo (billed annually) for 1 user. Core does not support additional user add-ons. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **Connect Tier Pricing**: Connect costs $139/mo (billed monthly) or $99/mo (billed annually) for 1 user, or $199/mo monthly for up to 5 users. Additional users beyond base counts cost $29/user/mo. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **Grow Tier Pricing**: Grow costs $249/mo (billed monthly) or $199/mo (billed annually) for up to 10 users ($349/mo monthly for larger team packs). Additional users past included pack cost $29/user/mo. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **Plus Tier Pricing**: Plus costs $499/mo (billed monthly) or $399/mo (billed annually) for up to 15 users. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **Feature Paywalling (Messaging & API)**: 2-way SMS texting requires Connect ($139+/mo) or higher. Open API access is paywalled to Plus ($499/mo) or select Grow add-ons. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/]
- **AI Copilot Capability**: Jobber Copilot provides AI-powered business coaching, proposal drafts, and operational recommendations. It is included free for U.S. and Canadian customers. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/hclp/ai-do-it-for-me/ / https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jobber-launches-copilot-the-first-of-several-ai-powered-products-aimed-at-making-home-service-business-ownership-simpler-than-ever-before-302264047.html]
- **AI Receptionist / Voice Capability**: Jobber Receptionist answers calls and texts 24/7, takes messages, and books work. Pricing is $29/mo add-on (includes 30 conversations, +$0.79/conversation overage) or $99/mo standard add-on (included free in Plus). [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/features/ai-receptionist/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai/]
- **G2 Rating & Count**: 4.6 out of 5 stars across ~530 verified reviews. [Source: https://www.g2.com/products/jobber/reviews]
- **Capterra Rating & Count**: 4.6 out of 5 stars across ~1,487 verified reviews. [Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/127994/Jobber/]
- **Apple App Store Rating**: 4.8 out of 5 stars across 20,000+ reviews. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/ / https://skillmammoth.com/blog/jobber-reviews]
- **Google Play Store Rating**: 4.7 out of 5 stars across 6,700+ reviews. [Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.getjobber.jobber]
- **Recent 2026 Changes**: Broad deployment of Jobber Receptionist (AI voice/chat add-on) and Jobber Copilot features; continued enforcement of $499/mo Plus tier for full open API access. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-ai-voice-and-chat-beta/]
- **#1 Complaint Theme**: QuickBooks Online sync errors/discrepancies and steep per-user scaling costs alongside gating 2-way SMS and open API behind high tiers. [Source: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/jobber/ / https://myquoteiq.com/jobbers-biggest-problem-exposed/]
