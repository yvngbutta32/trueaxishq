## Facts

- **Payments - ACH & Instant Payouts**: ACH bank payments supported via HCP Payments (1% fee, 2-7 business day payout); Instapay provides instant payouts to debit/bank (+1% fee) in minutes 24/7. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/2046930-housecall-pro-payment-processing-options / https://www.housecallpro.com/features/instapay/]
- **Payments - BNPL & Surcharging**: Wisetack consumer financing integration ($500–$65,000, 3–120 mo terms, 0–35.9% APR, flat 3.9% merchant fee); native Credit Card Surcharging tool automatically adds card processing fees at checkout with state compliance caps. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/4720493-wisetack-consumer-lending-overview / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/14543891-credit-card-surcharging-faq]
- **Payments - Deposit Collection**: Online deposit collection on estimates and online booking widgets prior to dispatch. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/online-booking-setup]
- **Scheduling - Route Optimization**: Route optimization via Beeline Routes integration and native routing tools available on Essentials ($149/mo) and MAX ($299/mo) plans. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5776530-beeline-routes-integration-overview]
- **Scheduling - Capacity & Multi-day**: Arrival window capacity settings and online booking slot throttling; multi-day job scheduling with parent-child sub-jobs and milestone invoicing. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/multi-day-jobs]
- **Scheduling - Recurring Services**: Recurring service agreements, automated job generation, and recurring customer billing. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/recurring-service-plans]
- **Client-Facing - Portal Capabilities**: Customer Web Portal allows online invoice/estimate approval (with Wisetack financing), payment processing, new job booking, and real-time tech GPS arrival tracking. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/customer-portal]
- **Client-Facing - Booking & Reviews**: Embeddable online booking engine with live calendar availability; automated post-job SMS/email review requests linking to Google, Facebook, and HCP profiles. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/features/online-booking/ / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/automated-review-requests]
- **AI Products & Pricing**: CSR AI / AI Team (24/7 automated call/text handling, lead qualification, automatic calendar booking) priced as a $99–$149/mo add-on or included in top-tier Pipeline/MAX packages. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/news-press/release-notes/]
- **Integrations - QuickBooks Sync**: Bi-directional sync with both QuickBooks Online (QBO) and QuickBooks Desktop (QBD), gated to Essentials ($149/mo) and MAX ($299/mo) tiers. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/quickbooks-desktop-integration-overview]
- **Integrations - API, Webhooks & Zapier**: Official Zapier app; REST Open API (default ~150 req/min rate limit) gated strictly to MAX plan ($299+/mo); native Webhooks app toggleable in App Store. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/8505035-api-overview / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/5683520-how-to-enable-webhooks]
- **Communication - SMS & Email**: 2-way customer texting included on Essentials ($149/mo) and MAX ($299/mo); automated Email Marketing campaign builder; automated "On My Way" SMS with live GPS tracking. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/ / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/automated-text-notifications]
- **Subcontractor & Crew Management**: Granular field tech sub-account permissions (restricts accounting/unassigned jobs); built-in internal team chat inside mobile/web apps; multi-tech crew scheduling on dispatch board. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/1073431-team-member-roles-permissions / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/team-chat]
