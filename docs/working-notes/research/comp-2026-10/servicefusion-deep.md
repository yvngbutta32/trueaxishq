# Service Fusion - Deep-Dive Research (October 2026)

## Facts

* **Signature System - Flat-Rate Unlimited-User Pricing**: Predictable tiered pricing (Starter $208/mo, Plus $325/mo, Pro $533/mo billed annually) with $0 per-user fees, granting unlimited user seats for office staff, field technicians, and subcontractors. Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing & https://opsler.com/compare/service-fusion/pricing/
* **Signature System - Bi-Directional QuickBooks Integration**: Deep two-way sync for both QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop/Enterprise, synchronizing customers, jobs, invoices, line items, inventory, and payroll hours without manual re-entry. Source: https://fieldserviceguide.com/servicefusion/ & https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing
* **Signature System - ServiceCall.ai Integrated Telephony**: Native cloud VoIP system featuring automatic caller history screen pops, call recording, AI call transcriptions, and instant call summaries linked directly to job files. Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/ai-call-answering-service & https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing
* **2025-2026 Weakness #1 - Mobile App Instability & Android Glitches**: Android mobile app (rated ~2.6–2.8 stars on Google Play) experiences frequent crashes, offline sync delays, lost field photo attachments, and slow job status updates. Source: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/service-fusion/ & https://onehousemarketing.com/service-fusion-review/
* **2025-2026 Weakness #2 - Outdated Desktop UI & Add-On Fee Creep**: Aging web dashboard interface with a steep learning curve, combined with cumulative add-on costs for core features like GPS fleet tracking ($20–$25/vehicle/mo) and ServiceCall.ai (~$150/mo). Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/135788/Service-Fusion/ & https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/service-fusion/
* **Most-Requested Feature #1 - Mobile Field App Redesign**: Fully overhauled, stable mobile app with offline reliability and fast photo/document sync speed. Source: https://onehousemarketing.com/service-fusion-review/ & https://www.capterra.com/p/135788/Service-Fusion/reviews/
* **Most-Requested Feature #2 - Advanced Native Inventory & PO Tracking**: Comprehensive inventory barcoding, purchase order workflows, and multi-warehouse stock transfer tracking. Source: https://onehousemarketing.com/service-fusion-review/
* **Most-Requested Feature #3 - Route Optimization & Dynamic Dispatching**: Intelligent multi-stop route optimization and live GPS fleet dispatching based on traffic conditions. Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/service-fusion-review
* **2026 Moves - Generative AI Telephony & FusionPay Updates**: Added generative AI transcriptions and sentiment analysis to ServiceCall.ai VoIP, alongside updated FusionPay embedded payment processing for ACH and instant card payouts. Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/ai-call-answering-service & https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing
* **Typical Customer Profile**: Mid-sized growing trade contractors (5 to 50+ technicians in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, commercial services) with expanding technician fleets who demand flat software costs without per-seat penalties and depend on bi-directional QuickBooks sync. Source: https://fieldserviceguide.com/servicefusion-2/ & https://www.contractorsoftwarehub.com/service-fusion-review/
