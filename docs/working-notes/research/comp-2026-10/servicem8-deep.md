# ServiceM8 Deep Dive Fact File (October 2026)

## Facts
* **Signature System - iOS Field UX**: Native iOS-exclusive design optimized for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch with Liquid Glass UI updates, offline mobile access, and Tap to Pay on iPhone integration (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/september-2025-update).
* **Signature System - QR Asset Check-Ins**: Asset Management add-on utilizes scannable QR code labels applied to customer equipment for instant field lookup, service history tracking, digital inspection forms, and automated compliance logging (Source: https://support.servicem8.com/help-center/servicem8-add-ons/asset-management/how-to-get-started-with-the-asset-management-add-on).
* **Signature System - Job-Volume Pricing**: Pricing structure offers unlimited user seats across all plans, charging strictly based on monthly completed job quotas (Free: $0/30 jobs, Starter: $29/50 jobs, Growing: $79/150 jobs, Premium: $149/500 jobs; $0.20 per overage job) (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/pricing).
* **2025-2026 Weakness #1 - Android Parity Gap**: Lack of a fully native, feature-complete Android app creates operational friction for teams with mixed mobile hardware, as the Android app lags behind iOS features (Source: https://fervorstudio.ca/news/servicem8-review-pricing-alternatives/).
* **2025-2026 Weakness #2 - Seasonal Cost Inflexibility**: Strict monthly job tier caps penalize busy seasonal periods with automatic plan tier jumps or usage fees ($0.20/job) when job counts spike (Source: https://myquoteiq.com/compare/servicem8/).
* **2025-2026 Weakness #3 - Complex Project Limits**: Limited native capabilities for managing multi-day projects, Gantt scheduling, and heavy parts inventory tracking compared to enterprise competitors (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/september-2025-update).
* **Most-Requested Feature #1 - Feature-Complete Android App**: High demand for native Android app feature parity with the iOS version for field technicians using Android devices (Source: https://fervorstudio.ca/news/servicem8-review-pricing-alternatives/).
* **Most-Requested Feature #2 - Native Multi-Day Project Scheduling**: Expanded Gantt-style scheduling, multi-stage job progress tracking, and dependency management for larger trade contracts (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/september-2025-update).
* **Most-Requested Feature #3 - In-Depth Inventory & Cost Tracking**: Built-in purchase order management, inventory serialization, and job costing beyond accounting system sync (Source: https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/servicem8-profile/reviews/).
* **2026 Moves - AI Phone Agent Launch**: Introduction of the ServiceM8 Phone Agent, a 24/7 AI virtual receptionist add-on ($49/mo) that answers incoming calls, collects client details, and automatically creates job cards (Source: https://support.servicem8.com/help-center/servicem8-add-ons/servicem8-phone-agent/introducing-the-servicem8-phone-agent).
* **2026 Moves - Tap to Pay & AI Workflow**: Integration of native Tap to Pay on iPhone and automated AI quoting/invoicing assistance across core plans (Source: https://www.servicem8.com/us/september-2025-update).
