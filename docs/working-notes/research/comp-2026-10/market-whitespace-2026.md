## Facts
- **Per-Seat Pricing Bloat**: Legacy FSM platforms charge $29–$400+/tech/mo, causing severe cost scaling bottlenecks for expanding SMB crews. (Source: https://resources.rework.com/tools/field-service/jobber-vs-housecall-pro)
- **Unreliable Mobile Offline Mode**: Techs lose notes and job photos in offline dead zones due to weak local queueing and sync conflicts. (Source: https://www.rishabhsoft.com/blog/offline-first-field-service-management-application)
- **Subcontractor Privacy & Permission Gaps**: Most SMB FSMs lack role-based access for 3rd-party crews, exposing internal margins or client contacts. (Source: https://www.praxedo.com/our-blog/improving-3rd-party-contractor-management-in-field-service/)
- **High Inbound Lead Drop-Off**: Over 40% of after-hours calls go unanswered, losing high-intent leads to fast-responding competitors. (Source: https://agent23.ai/industry/hvac)
- **Add-On Paywall Fatigue**: Essential tools like SMS marketing, AI answering, and reviews are locked behind $29–$99+/mo add-on tiers. (Source: https://myquoteiq.com/jobber-pricing-breakdown-2025/)
- **Switch Driver — Unannounced Rate Hikes**: Aggressive annual price hikes and contract lock-ins are the top trigger for switching platforms. (Source: https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/servicetitan-vs-housecall-pro)
- **Switch Driver — Tech Mobile Rejection**: Over 45% of field techs find FSM mobile apps slow or overly complex, driving operational churn. (Source: https://fieldservicesoftware.io/blog/field-service-management-software-statistics/)
- **Switch Driver — Scaling Tier Surges**: Adding 2–3 techs forces SMBs into enterprise tiers, increasing monthly costs by 3x–5x. (Source: https://www.getonecrew.com/post/jobber-pricing)
- **AI Production — 24/7 Answering Conversion**: Voice AI receptionists convert 25%–30% of after-hours/overflow calls directly into booked jobs. (Source: https://www.pitchit.ai/ai-receptionist/ai-receptionist-for-hvac-companies)
- **AI Production — Automated SMS Workflows**: Instant SMS appointment and quote follow-ups deliver proven ROI and higher booking rates. (Source: https://contractorplus.app/blog/housecall-pro-vs-jobber-vs-servicetitan)
- **AI Hype — Fully Autonomous Dispatch**: AI routing without human supervisor overrides fails under live traffic or inventory realities. (Source: https://www.panorama-consulting.com/why-field-service-management-systems-fail-and-how-to-avoid-it/)
- **AI Hype — Photo Estimating**: Photo-based AI auto-quoting yields high error rates on complex trade jobs, causing contractor margin loss. (Source: https://gigacatalyst.com/blog/field-service-ai-features-2026)
- **Subcontractor Coordination Status**: BuildOps and Fieldwire offer basic features, but mainstream SMB tools lack multi-crew workflows. (Source: https://buildops.com/resources/subcontractor-management-software)
- **ServiceTitan Enterprise M&A**: ServiceTitan (TTAN, ~$961M FY26 rev) acquired Conduit Tech (HVAC, Sept 2025) and Convex (CRM) to lock in enterprise trades. (Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/press/servicetitan-hvac-acquisition-conduit-tech)
- **Threat — Vertical AI Agent Platforms**: Standalone AI voice/dispatch agents (Dapta, Agent23) threaten incumbent FSMs by capturing intake. (Source: https://gettinylawn.com/best/ai-receptionist-for-lawn-care/)
- **Reputation Management Bundling**: Winners bundle basic post-job SMS review requests, but legacy FSMs push users to expensive add-ons. (Source: https://itsupportdavenport.com/servicetitan-vs-housecall-pro-vs-jobber-the-it-side-and-the-florida-call-recording-problem/)
