# Service Fusion Competitor Research (October 2026)

## Overview
Service Fusion is a field service management (FSM) software designed for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and light commercial contractors, featuring flat-rate pricing with unlimited users.

## Facts
* **Pricing Tiers**: Starter is $208/mo (billed annually) or $245/mo (billed monthly); Plus is $325/mo (billed annually) or $382/mo (billed monthly); Pro is $533/mo (billed annually) or $627/mo (billed monthly). One-time implementation fee is $199–$399. Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing
* **User Costs & Contracts**: $0 per additional user (unlimited users on all tiers). Offers month-to-month options or annual contracts with a 15% discount. Source: https://opsler.com/compare/service-fusion/pricing/
* **Paywalled Features**: Basic scheduling/dispatching in Starter. Customer SMS text messaging, customer portal, job costing, and inventory are paywalled to Plus ($325/mo). Open REST API access is paywalled to Pro ($533/mo). GPS fleet tracking is a paid add-on ($20–$25/vehicle/mo). Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing
* **AI Capabilities**: Offers ServiceCall.ai VoIP add-on (~$150/mo) featuring automated call answering, AI call transcription, and call summaries. Does not include a fully autonomous AI voice receptionist or core FSM generative assistant. Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/ai-call-answering-service
* **Integrations & Payments**: Bi-directional integration with QuickBooks (Online and Desktop) and Zapier. FusionPay gateway supports credit card and ACH processing, with standard merchant payouts. Source: https://www.servicefusion.com/pricing
* **Reputation & Ratings**: G2 rating is 4.1/5 stars (~124 reviews). Capterra rating is 4.3/5 stars (~308 reviews). iOS App Store rating is 4.4–4.6/5 stars (~1,200+ ratings). Android Google Play rating is 2.6–2.8/5 stars (~800+ reviews). Source: https://www.g2.com/products/service-fusion/reviews
* **#1 Complaint**: Field worker mobile app instability on Android (frequent crashes, offline sync delays, lost job photos/data) and accumulative costs of add-ons (GPS tracking and ServiceCall.ai). Source: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/service-fusion/
* **Standout Strength**: Flat-rate pricing model with unlimited user seats combined with robust bi-directional QuickBooks sync. Source: https://www.contractorsoftwarehub.com/service-fusion-review/
* **Target Segment**: Small to mid-market field service companies (3–50+ technicians in HVAC, plumbing, electrical). Source: https://fieldserviceguide.com/servicefusion-2/
