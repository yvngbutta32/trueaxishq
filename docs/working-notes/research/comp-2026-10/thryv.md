# Thryv Competitor Research (October 2026)

## Overview
Thryv is an all-in-one small business management and marketing suite designed for local service providers, offering CRM, reputation management, online booking, and multi-channel communication tools.

## Facts
* **Pricing Tiers**: Command Center offers Free ($0/mo), Plus ($20/user/mo), and Professional ($30/user/mo). Business Center standalone plans range from Starter ($99/mo) to Essential/Plus ($185–$244/mo) and Signature/Accelerate ($399–$646/mo+). One-time onboarding fees range from $250–$490. Source: https://www.thryv.com/pricing/
* **User Costs & Contracts**: Additional seats cost $20–$30/user/mo depending on center. Standard contracts require 6 to 18-month commitments auto-renewing; annual payment discounts average 10–15%. Source: https://saasrat.com/products/thryv
* **Paywalled Features**: 2-way texting and centralized inbox are available in Command Center / Business Center; mass broadcast marketing requires Marketing Center. Online booking and client portal require paid Business Center plans. Advanced reporting and open API access require Professional/Signature tiers. GPS fleet tracking is not natively built. Source: https://learn.thryv.com/hc/en-us/articles/40681165842189-Thryv-Products-at-a-glance-US
* **AI Capabilities**: Includes an AI assistant for auto-drafting message responses, review replies, and client portfolio insights. No native AI voice receptionist (requires 3rd-party integrations like Abby Connect or Viva AI). Source: https://www.thryv.com/blog/ai-customer-service-software/
* **Integrations & Payments**: Integrates with QuickBooks (Online/Desktop) and Zapier. ThryvPay processes payments with flat credit fees and 1% capped ($9) ACH, supporting Credit, ACH, BNPL (Klarna/PayPal), and next-day/instant payouts. Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thryv.thryvpay
* **Reputation & Ratings**: G2 rating is 4.2/5 stars (~2,050 reviews). Capterra rating is 4.2/5 stars (~538 reviews). iOS App Store rating is 3.7/5 stars (~280 reviews). Android Google Play rating is 3.1/5 stars (~284 reviews for Business Center app). Source: https://www.g2.com/sellers/thryv
* **#1 Complaint**: Strict 6–18 month contracts with automatic renewals, high cancellation fees, and aggressive sales/onboarding tactics. Source: https://skillmammoth.com/blog/thryv-reviews
* **Standout Strength**: Centralized multi-channel customer communication inbox (Command Center) paired with automated reputation management and online listing management. Source: https://www.g2.com/products/thryv/reviews
* **Target Segment**: Micro to small businesses (1–20 employees / local service contractors and retail). Source: https://checkthat.ai/brands/thryv/pricing
