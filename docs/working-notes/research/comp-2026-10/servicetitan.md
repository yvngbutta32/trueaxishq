# ServiceTitan Competitor Fact File (October 2026)

## Facts
* **Pricing Model & Rates**: Official pricing remains unlisted publicly (quote-based via demo; https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing). Current 2026 contractor pricing ranges from $245 to $500+ per technician per month (Starter: ~$245–$300/tech/mo; Essentials: ~$300–$400/tech/mo; Pro/Enterprise: ~$400–$500+/tech/mo) (Source: https://procured.us/articles/servicetitan-pricing, https://projul.com/blog/servicetitan-pricing-analysis-2026/).
* **Implementation & Contract Terms**: One-time onboarding/implementation fee ranges from $5,000 to $50,000+ depending on company size. Requires mandatory 12- to 36-month minimum annual contracts with strict auto-renewal terms (Source: https://servicestorm.io/articles/servicetitan-pricing, https://tooleduppro.com/guides/servicetitan-pricing/).
* **AI Capabilities (Titan Intelligence)**: Titan Intelligence (TI) suite powers AI Virtual Agent / AI Voice Agent for automated phone answering, scheduling, and dispatch routing (Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/features/pro/virtual-agent, https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/ai-voice-agent-for-basic-phones-and-phones-pro-faq).
* **AI Pricing**: AI Voice Virtual Agent is usage-based starting at ~$2.75 per call, with volume discount tiers available (Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/podcasts/mastering-servicetitan/henry-cheng-interview).
* **AI Features**: Includes AI Smart Dispatch, Atlas demand-based scheduling, automated invoice review, and benchmark pricing analytics (Source: https://www.usecarly.com/blog/best-ai-automation-tools-servicetitan/).
* **G2 Rating**: 4.4 out of 5 stars based on ~374 verified reviews (Source: https://www.g2.com/products/servicetitan/reviews).
* **Capterra Rating**: 4.3 out of 5 stars based on ~342 verified reviews (Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/150053/ServiceTitan/alternatives/).
* **iOS App Store Ratings**: ServiceTitan Field app holds 3.5 out of 5 stars across 5.3K ratings (Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/servicetitan-field/id1629928933); legacy ServiceTitan Mobile holds 2.9 out of 5 stars across 1.7K ratings (Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/servicetitan-mobile/id1037989976).
* **Google Play Ratings**: ServiceTitan Field holds 3.5 out of 5 stars across 695 reviews (Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.servicetitan.work); ServiceTitan Mobile holds 2.5 out of 5 stars across 1.04K reviews (Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.servicetitan.mobile).
* **2026 Product & Pricing Changes**: Mandatory transition toward the new "ServiceTitan Field" mobile app and expanded agentic TI workflows (AI Virtual Agent and Atlas accounting/dispatch automation) (Source: https://www.g2.com/products/servicetitan/reviews, https://www.reddit.com/r/HVAC/comments/1nyv5j1/new_servicetitan_app_is_a_downgrade/).
* **#1 Complaint Theme**: Excessive overall cost ($5k–$50k onboarding + high monthly per-tech fees) locked into multi-year binding contracts, coupled with bugginess/slowness in the forced new mobile app rollout (Source: https://contractortoolstack.com/software/servicetitan/, https://www.reddit.com/r/HVAC/comments/1nyv5j1/new_servicetitan_app_is_a_downgrade/).
