# Housecall Pro - Deep-Dive Research (October 2026)

## Facts

- **Sales Builder Proposal System**: Sales Builder allows HVAC, plumbing, and trade pros to build visual Good-Better-Best proposals on web and mobile; presents side-by-side package choices, custom imagery, line-item upsells, and digital signature capture directly during customer presentations. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/features/sales-proposal-tool/ / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/7834730-sales-proposal-tool-on-web]
- **CSR AI / AI Team Capabilities**: Customer Service Rep AI (CSR AI) answers incoming calls and web chats 24/7, qualifies customer leads, schedules jobs directly into HCP dispatch calendars, and sends automated booking confirmations. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/news-press/release-notes/fall-is-just-around-the-corner-keep-the-momentum-going-with-our-new-features/ / https://www.stork.ai/en/housecall-pro]
- **Mobile Field App Strengths**: Mobile app for iOS/Android features real-time job notifications, automated "On My Way" SMS with GPS arrival tracking, mobile pricebook lookup, visual proposal presentation, photo/video attachments, signature capture, and tap-to-pay payment processing. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/features/mobile-app/ / https://apps.apple.com/us/app/housecall-pro-field-service/id692833651]
- **Product Weakness - Android App Lag & Sync Bugs**: Android users report higher crash rates, UI latency during peak operational hours, and sync delays compared to the iOS application. [Source: https://contractortoolstack.com/software/housecall-pro/ / https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=housecall.pros]
- **Product Weakness - QuickBooks Sync Tiering & Errors**: Two-way QuickBooks sync is paywalled to Essentials ($149+/mo) and MAX ($299+/mo) plans, and users report duplicate transactions and sync disconnects. [Source: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/housecall-pro/ / https://www.capterra.com/p/140363/HouseCall-Pro/]
- **Product Weakness - Payment Payout Holds & Gated API**: Merchant processing (HCP Payments) faces complaints over security payout holds and dispute delays, while Open API access is gated strictly to MAX ($299+/mo). [Source: https://tradesbusinesssoftware.com/reviews/housecall-pro-review / https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **Most-Requested Features**: Advanced native inventory tracking with purchase orders, enhanced two-way QuickBooks sync conflict resolution controls, multi-location/sub-account management, and granular technician permissions. [Source: https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/10828735-hcp-pro-vote-faqs / https://contractortoolstack.com/software/housecall-pro/]
- **Onboarding Time-to-Value**: Core setup takes 1-5 days for basic scheduling and payments, but full implementation (pricebook, QBO, proposal templates) requires 1-2 weeks. [Source: https://axisconsulting.io/is-housecall-pro-worth-it/]
- **Support Quality Reputation**: In-app chat support is praised for quick response times (<1-2 minutes), but phone support access is gated to upper plans and payment hold resolutions can be slow. [Source: https://www.trustpilot.com/review/housecallpro.com / https://www.facebook.com/groups/727874762768935/posts/1127906716099069/]
- **2026 Product Launches**: May 2026 UI/UX platform refresh introduced tab-based job organization, route-based scheduling, automated sales proposal updates, job photo reports, and integrated payroll time tracking (Feb 2026 release). [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/may-2026-product-updates/ / https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/13975339-platform-refresh-changelog]
