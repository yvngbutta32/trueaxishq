# ServiceTitan Feature Matrix (October 2026)

## Facts
* **Payments**: credit card, ACH, and check collection from office and field; "Pay by Bank" lets customers authorize payments directly from their banking app, avoiding ACH returns (Source: https://www.servicetitan.com/features/payments).
* **Consumer financing**: Affirm is a financing partner for BNPL, offered by technicians in the field AND in the Customer Portal; ServiceTitan data shows contractors offering financing see ~12% higher close rates and ~13% higher average ticket sizes (Sources: https://help.servicetitan.com/release-hub/docs/offer-affirm-financing-to-give-homeowners-more-ways-to-pay-for-your-services, https://www.servicetitan.com/press/servicetitan-consumer-trends-report-homeowners).
* **Customer Portal**: homeowner-facing portal with online payments and Affirm financing options (Source: https://help.servicetitan.com/release-hub/docs/offer-affirm-financing-in-the-customer-portal-to-help-homeowners-pay-for-jobs).
* **Open API**: public developer portal (developer.servicetitan.io) with admin-gated app creation; part of enterprise purchase, no published rate limits (Sources: https://help.servicetitan.com/docs/get-started-with-api-dev-portal-v2, https://developer.servicetitan.io/).
* **Pricing reality 2026**: $245–$500 per tech per month + $5,000–$50,000 implementation; year one for a 10-tech shop can reach $60k+ (Sources: https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/servicetitan/, https://www.fieldproxy.ai/resources/blog/servicetitan-pricing-vs-competitor-pricing).
* **Entry barrier**: mandatory long onboarding (2–16 weeks documented), contracts, account-manager-mediated setup (Source: docs/working-notes/research/comp-2026-10/servicetitan-deep.md).
