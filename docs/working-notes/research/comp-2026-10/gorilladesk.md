# GorillaDesk Competitor Research (October 2026)

## Facts

* **Pricing Tiers & Terms**: Basic plan is $49/mo (1st route/schedule), Pro plan is $99/mo (1st route), and Growth plan is $149/mo (1st route) billed monthly; extra routes cost $30–$50/mo on Basic, $50/mo on Pro, and $50–$100/mo on Growth. Unlimited admin/office/crew users are included free (pricing is strictly per solo technician route). No setup fees or long-term contracts (month-to-month). Annual billing provides 1 month free (~8.33% discount). Source: https://gorilladesk.com/pricing/
* **Feature Paywall Breakdown**:
  - *Basic ($49/mo)*: Invoicing, estimates, basic routing (up to 25 stops), Square & Stripe payment integration, automated email/SMS payment reminders, chemical/material tracking, 20 standard reports.
  - *Pro ($99/mo)*: Customer online portal, online booking, Stripe/Square subscription billing, Drive Matrix routing, review generation, e-signatures, GPS device tracking & trap barcoding, QuickBooks Online sync, Zapier integration, Email inbox sync, and Open API access.
  - *Growth ($149/mo)*: Map estimation tools, estimated drive time display, Best Available Time AI scheduling, Job Magnet route compaction, sales pipeline/opportunity tracking, multi-branch support.
  Source: https://gorilladesk.com/pricing/ & https://gorilladesk.com/pricing-3/comparison/
* **AI Capabilities**: Offers an integrated VoIP AI Receptionist / AI Agent that answers inbound calls, SMS, web chat, and client portal requests 24/7 to answer customer queries, collect leads, and auto-schedule jobs directly onto technician calendars. Includes AI-driven route optimization ("Best Available Time" & "Job Magnet") on Growth. SMS/VoIP requires a $5/mo line subscription plus prepaid outbound credit packages ($50 for 1,000 texts up to $200 for 8,000 texts). Source: https://gorilladesk.com/features/ai-agents/ & https://gorilladesk.com/pricing/
* **Integrations & Payments**: Integrates natively with QuickBooks Online (Pro+), Zapier (Pro+), Open API (Pro+), Stripe, and Square. Supports credit card and ACH processing through Stripe/Square; standard instant payouts and payment methods via Stripe without separate native BNPL features. Source: https://gorilladesk.com/pricing/
* **Reputation & Ratings**:
  - G2 Rating: 4.8 / 5 stars (~43 reviews). Source: https://www.g2.com/products/gorilladesk/competitors/alternatives
  - Capterra Rating: 4.8 / 5 stars (~278 reviews). Source: https://www.capterra.com/compare/130290-160267/GorillaDesk-vs-Briostack
  - iOS App Store Rating: 4.4 / 5 stars (62 ratings). Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gorilladesk/id978627591
  - Google Play Rating: ~4.2 / 5 stars (~406 reviews). Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gorilladesk.app
* **Complaint Theme & Standout Strength**:
  - *#1 Complaint Theme*: SMS add-on costs ($5/mo fee plus per-text credit packages) and gating essential operational tools (QuickBooks sync, Zapier, customer portal, GPS tracking) behind the $99/mo Pro tier.
  - *Standout Strength*: Industry-leading customer support with free data migration and an extremely clean, easy-to-use interface with unlimited free admin seats.
  Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/130290/GorillaDesk/reviews/ & https://fieldserviceguide.com/gorilladesk/
* **Target Segment**: Micro businesses to small crews (1–15 technicians), specializing in pest control, lawn care, pool service, and residential service operations. Source: https://fieldserviceguide.com/gorilladesk/
