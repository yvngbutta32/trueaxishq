# FieldPulse - Competitor Fact Sheet (October 2026)

## Overview
FieldPulse is a seat-based field service management (FSM) software designed for trade service contractors in HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing, and commercial equipment.

## Facts
* **Pricing Tiers**: Seat-based quote-only pricing customized per team. Official pricing requires getting a custom quote (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing). Third-party benchmarks report user seats range from ~$65 to $130+/user/month (commonly ~$89–$99/user/mo for full access, with lower rates for field-only seats and annual contract discounts) (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
* **Feature Paywalls**:
  * Core Platform: Scheduling & dispatch, job/work order management, estimates & invoices, customer portal, booking portal, basic reporting, QuickBooks sync, and Maverick AI assistant (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
  * Premium Add-ons / Tiers: Paywalls Engage (built-in phone/SMS system), Operator AI (24/7 AI call dispatcher), Fleet Tracking, Custom Forms, and Open API (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
* **AI Capabilities**:
  * Maverick: Embedded AI assistant included on every plan for back-office reporting and workflow analysis (https://www.fieldpulse.com/features/maverick).
  * Operator AI: 24/7 AI Voice Receptionist / Dispatcher that answers incoming phone calls, captures leads, and books jobs (sold as paid add-on) (https://www.fieldpulse.com/features/operator-ai).
  * Chat AI: Automated AI messaging integrated into customer communications (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
* **Integrations & Payments**: Direct integration with QuickBooks (Online & Desktop) and Zapier; Open API available on enterprise setup (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing). FieldPulse Payments handles credit card processing, mobile point-of-sale, ACH, FieldPulse Financing (BNPL options for customer quotes), and instant payout settlement (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
* **Reputation & Ratings**:
  * G2: 4.7/5 stars (~376 reviews) (https://www.g2.com/products/fieldpulse-fieldpulse/reviews).
  * Capterra: 4.6/5 stars (~506 reviews) (https://www.capterra.com/p/153475/FieldPulse/).
  * iOS App Store: 4.0/5 stars (~117 ratings for main app; ~4.5/5 for Engage app) (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fieldpulse/id1575955293).
  * Google Play Store: ~4.1/5 stars (~100+ ratings) (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fieldpulse.app).
* **Complaint vs. Strength**:
  * #1 Complaint: Mobile app UI/performance bugs and occasional QuickBooks sync errors (duplicate entries / sync mismatches) (https://www.g2.com/products/fieldpulse-fieldpulse/reviews).
  * Standout Strength: Deep workflow customization via ClearPath and advanced multi-option estimating / pricebooks (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
* **Target Segment**: Small-to-mid market trade contractors (3–50+ tech crews in HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and commercial services) (https://www.fieldpulse.com/pricing).
