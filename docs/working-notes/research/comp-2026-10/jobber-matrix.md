## Facts

- **Payments - ACH & Instant Payouts**: Supports ACH bank payments via Jobber Payments (1% fee, capped at $15 or standard processing; 2-5 business days) and Instant Payouts via Stripe (+1% payout fee, instant deposit 24/7). [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/bank-payments-ach/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/instant-payouts/]
- **Payments - BNPL & Surcharging**: Integrated consumer financing via Wisetack (loans $500–$25,000+, 3–60 mo terms, 0–35.9% APR, 3.9%+ merchant fee); lacks native automated credit card surcharging, requiring manual fee line items or tax workarounds. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-wisetack-consumer-financing-integration/ / https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/invoicing-getting-paid/advice-adding-the-credit-card-service-fee-to-invoices/1228]
- **Payments - Deposit Collection**: Supports mandatory online deposit collection on quotes and online booking requests prior to job scheduling. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/online-booking/]
- **Scheduling - Route Optimization**: Native master route and daily route optimization included in Connect ($139/mo) and Plus ($499/mo) plans. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/route-optimization-new-schedule/]
- **Scheduling - Capacity & Multi-day**: Basic calendar visit limits without ML capacity forecasting; Jobber Projects (2026) supports multi-day/multi-visit job tracking and milestone billing. [Source: https://productupdates.getjobber.com/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/calendar-settings/]
- **Scheduling - Recurring Services**: Native recurring jobs and contract maintenance schedules with automatic visit generation. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/recurring-jobs/]
- **Client-Facing - Portal Capabilities**: Client Hub offers 24/7 quote approvals (with add-ons/financing), invoice payments, appointment requests, real-time tech GPS tracking, and receipt printing (no live 2-way chat inside Hub). [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/what-do-your-clients-see-in-client-hub/]
- **Client-Facing - Booking & Reviews**: Embeddable online booking pages with required deposit collection; automated review requests via Jobber Payments post-transaction. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/online-booking/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-payments-reviews/]
- **AI Products & Pricing**: Jobber Copilot (free operational coach and AI quote description generator for US/CA) and Receptionist AI (24/7 AI call/text answering, lead intake, job booking; $29/mo add-on for 30 conversations or free on Plus $499/mo). [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/receptionistpowered-by-jobber-ai/]
- **Integrations - QuickBooks Sync**: Bi-directional sync with QuickBooks Online (QBO) for clients, invoices, payments, and items; QuickBooks Desktop (QBD) is NOT supported. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quickbooks-integration-faqs-new-quickbooks-integration/]
- **Integrations - API, Webhooks & Zapier**: Official Zapier integration; GraphQL Open API with Rack::Attack rate limits (2,500 req / 5 min, 10,000 pts limit) gated strictly to Plus ($499/mo); native Webhooks supported via Developer Center. [Source: https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/using_jobbers_api/api_rate_limits/ / https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/using_jobbers_api/setting_up_webhooks/]
- **Communication - SMS & Email**: 2-way SMS included in Connect ($139/mo) and Plus ($499/mo) via Jobber Messenger; Jobber Campaigns for email marketing; automated customizable visit/invoice reminders. [Source: https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/ / https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-campaigns/]
- **Subcontractor & Crew Management**: Granular Worker/Subcontractor permissions (hides financials/quotes); in-app Team @Mentions in job notes with read receipts; multi-crew/multi-tech job assignment. [Source: https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/user-permissions/ / https://productupdates.getjobber.com/]
