# Housecall Pro - Competitor Analysis (October 2026)

## Overview
Housecall Pro is a leading FSM software platform for small-to-midsize home service contractors and trade crews.

## Facts

- **Official Pricing Page**: Housecall Pro offers 3 primary pricing tiers based on team size and feature depth. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **Basic Tier Pricing**: Basic costs $79/mo (billed monthly) or $59/mo (billed annually). Includes 1 user. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **Essentials Tier Pricing**: Essentials costs $189/mo (billed monthly) or $149/mo (billed annually). Includes up to 5 users. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **MAX Tier Pricing**: MAX costs $329/mo (billed monthly) or $299/mo (billed annually). Includes up to 8 users. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **Per-User Costs Past Base**: Additional users beyond 8 seats on MAX cost $35/user/mo. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **API Gating**: Open API access is gated strictly to the MAX tier ($329/mo). [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/max/]
- **AI Capabilities (CSR AI / AI Team)**: Customer Service Rep AI (CSR AI) provides 24/7 automated call answering and chat booking for trade contractors. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/features/ai-team/csr-ai/ / https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/]
- **AI Extra Costs**: CSR AI is an optional paid add-on module within Settings > AI Team for accounts starting on Basic ($59/mo+). [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/features/ai-team/]
- **G2 Rating & Count**: 4.3 out of 5 stars across ~190 verified reviews. [Source: https://www.g2.com/products/housecall-pro/reviews]
- **Capterra Rating & Count**: 4.7 out of 5 stars across ~2,700 verified reviews. [Source: https://www.capterra.com/p/140363/HouseCall-Pro/]
- **Apple App Store Rating**: 4.6 out of 5 stars across 22,000+ reviews. [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/]
- **Google Play Store Rating**: ~3.5 to 4.5 out of 5 stars (notably lower than iOS due to Android stability feedback). [Source: https://contractortoolstack.com/software/housecall-pro/ / https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=housecall.pros]
- **Recent 2026 Changes**: Wider integration of CSR AI across all tiers as an add-on; per-user add-on fee above 8 seats confirmed at $35/mo (down from historical estimates). [Source: https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/january-2025-product-release/]
- **#1 Complaint Theme**: Application latency/slowness during peak operational hours, Android app glitches, and occasional automated customer payout holds/refund friction on credit card processing. [Source: https://tradesbusinesssoftware.com/reviews/housecall-pro-review / https://onehousemarketing.com/housecall-pro-review/]
