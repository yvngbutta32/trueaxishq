# Workiz - Competitor Fact Sheet (October 2026)

## Overview
Workiz is a field service management (FSM) platform tailored for small-to-midsize service businesses and franchises in lock, lawn, HVAC, plumbing, and cleaning.

## Facts
* **Pricing Tiers**: Workiz offers 3 main tiers — Standard (growing teams), Pro (mid-size teams), and Ultimate (large teams/franchises). Official rates require custom quotes via "Request pricing" form (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/). Third-party benchmarks report Standard/Pro base starts ~$187–$229/mo (includes up to 5 seats; extra seats ~$55–$65/mo with annual billing discount) (https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/workiz-profile/).
* **Feature Paywalls**:
  * Standard: Unlimited jobs/invoices, scheduling, client portal, built-in reports, QuickBooks Online sync, Zapier, 2-way texting, 5 automations (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/).
  * Pro: Adds performance pay, custom reports, Genius leads/scheduling, 10 automations, multi-location roles (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/).
  * Ultimate: Adds service plans/memberships, inventory management, flat rate, checklists, multi-day jobs, equipment tracking, purchase orders, Open API, GPS/fleet tracking, 30 automations (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/).
* **AI Capabilities**: Features the Genius Suite, including Genius Answering (24/7 AI voice receptionist answering calls/SMS in EN/ES/FR with live transfer), Genius Leads & Scheduling (AI lead scoring & route optimization), Genius Call Insights, and Genius Marketing (AI ad writer/designer) (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/). AI receptionist & marketing sold as separate paid add-ons.
* **Integrations & Payments**: Integrates with QuickBooks Online and Zapier; Open API on Ultimate (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/). Workiz Pay handles card processing, ACH, estimate deposits, card readers, consumer financing (BNPL), and instant payout options (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/).
* **Reputation & Ratings**:
  * G2: 4.5/5 stars (~238 reviews) (https://www.g2.com/products/workiz/reviews).
  * Capterra: 4.4/5 stars (218 reviews) (https://www.capterra.com/p/147525/Workiz/).
  * iOS App Store: 4.6/5 stars (~2.2k ratings) (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/workiz-field-service-software/id1469769810).
  * Google Play Store: ~4.1/5 stars (~500+ ratings) (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.workiz).
* **Complaint vs. Strength**:
  * #1 Complaint: Opaque quote-only pricing, aggressive sales practices, and unexpected fees for telephony, SMS, and Genius AI add-ons (https://www.softwareadvice.com/field-service/workiz-profile/).
  * Standout Strength: Native integrated VoIP phone system (Genius Phone) and 24/7 AI voice receptionist (Genius Answering) embedded in dispatch (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/).
* **Target Segment**: Micro businesses (1–5 techs) up to mid-market and multi-location franchises (1–200 employees) (https://www.workiz.com/pricing-plans/).
