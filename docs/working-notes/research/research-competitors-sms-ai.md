## Executive Summary
This research analyzes how major Field Service Management (FSM) platforms (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, ServiceM8, Buildertrend) and standalone AI receptionist products package, price, and tier SMS texting and AI voice receptionist features. 

The primary strategic takeaway for TrueAxis HQ is that **two-way SMS texting is heavily paywalled by market leaders behind high tiers ($249–$329/mo)**, while **AI voice receptionists remain expensive add-ons ($99–$199/mo extra)** or standalone enterprise tools ($200–$500/mo). TrueAxis HQ has a high-value opportunity to disrupt the market by offering **bundled two-way SMS and AI voice receptionist features at $49–$149/mo**, specifically tailored for solo freelancers and small 1–3 person home-service teams.

## 1. Jobber
*Source Verified: October 2026 (getjobber.com, TradePicked, ContractorStack HQ, MarTechEdge, G2)*

### Plans & Pricing Tiers (Annual vs. Monthly)
* **Core:** $29/mo (billed annually) or $49/mo (billed monthly) — 1 user. Includes basic scheduling, quoting, invoicing, online payments, and client hub.
* **Connect:** $99/mo (billed annually) or $139–$169/mo (billed monthly) — up to 5 users ($29/mo per additional user). Includes automated appointment reminders, one-way SMS/email client notifications, QuickBooks Online sync, and GPS tracking.
* **Grow:** $249/mo (billed annually) or $299–$399/mo (billed monthly) — up to 15 users. Includes **Two-Way Text Messaging**, custom automations, line-item markups, and quote add-ons.
* **Teams Plus / Plus:** $499/mo (billed annually) or $699/mo (billed monthly) — up to 15–30 users. Includes Marketing Suite, dedicated account manager, and VIP support.

### Two-Way Texting & SMS Marketing
* **Two-Way Texting Tier:** Paywalled behind the **Grow plan ($249+/mo)**. Clients on Core or Connect can only send one-way automated transactional texts (appointment confirmations, arrival alerts).
* **SMS Marketing / Drip Campaigns:** Requires the **Marketing Suite add-on ($99/mo)** on Core, Connect, and Grow (included on Plus). Enables automated quote follow-ups, post-job review requests via SMS, and postcard/email campaigns.
* **Per-Message & Phone Number Fees:** Jobber includes a dedicated business phone number on Grow. There are no additional per-message fees charged by Jobber for standard two-way text conversations on Grow, though users must complete mandatory A2P 10DLC registration.

### AI Features (Jobber Copilot & AI Receptionist / Voice)
* **Jobber Copilot:** AI assistant integrated into the platform serving as a business coach, marketing copywriter, data analyst, and draft quote generator.
* **Jobber AI Receptionist / Jobber Voice:** Launched as an AI voice call-answering feature (~$99/month add-on or integrated via Marketplace partners such as XApp AI / OpenMic). Answers incoming calls, handles common questions, takes message details, and schedules appointments directly onto the Jobber calendar.

## 2. Housecall Pro
*Source Verified: October 2026 (housecallpro.com, LogiCatalog, UsedDeepSel, FieldTechReviews, Capterra)*

### Plans & Pricing Tiers (Annual vs. Monthly)
* **Basic:** $59/mo (billed annually) or $79/mo (billed monthly) — 1 user. Core dispatching, estimates, invoicing, online booking, payment processing.
* **Essentials:** $149/mo (billed annually) or $189/mo (billed monthly) — up to 5 users. Online booking, automated review requests, basic automated marketing.
* **MAX:** $299/mo (billed annually) or $329–$349/mo (billed monthly) — 1 to 8 users (custom beyond). Includes **Two-Way Texting (SMS Chat Center)**, advanced reporting, open API, lead management, priority support.

### Two-Way Texting & SMS Marketing
* **Two-Way Texting Tier:** Strictly paywalled behind the **MAX tier ($299–$329+/mo)**. Basic and Essentials plans are restricted to automated one-way client notifications (en-route texts, job confirmation).
* **SMS Marketing & Drip Campaigns:** Automated SMS follow-ups and drip marketing require HCP's "Automate" package or higher-tier marketing add-on modules ($99–$199/mo).

### AI Receptionist ('Eddie' / HCP AI Voice Assistant)
* **Offering:** Known as "Eddie the AI Assistant" or HCP AI Voice Answering. Provides 24/7 call answering for missed or after-hours calls.
* **Capabilities:** Greets callers, conducts customer intake (name, address, trade issue), checks technician schedule availability, and logs lead cards or books appointments directly into Housecall Pro.
* **Pricing Model:** Sold as an add-on module to Essentials and MAX (typically ~$99–$199/month base subscription, or bundled into MAX with metered monthly call caps). Overage charges apply if monthly call minutes/volume are exceeded.

## 3. ServiceTitan
*Source Verified: October 2026 (insights.velocityaipartners.co, ServiceTitan Pro Products Guide, SaaSworthy, ITQlick)*

### AI Voice Agent Offering
* **Product:** **Virtual Agent / Voice Agent** (powered by Titan Intelligence).
* **Capabilities:** Answers inbound calls 24/7 with natural conversational AI. Identifies calling customers via phone lookup, performs real-time address validation, checks technician availability on the dispatch board, books jobs directly into ServiceTitan, sends SMS confirmation, and executes warm call transfers to human CSRs for complex emergencies.

### Target Market & Enterprise Pricing Model
* **Target Segment:** Exclusively **mid-market to enterprise** home-service contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical with 5+ to 100+ technicians).
* **Base Platform Cost:** Starts at **$245 to $398 per technician/month**, with multi-technician contract minimums and onboarding fees ($5,000–$25,000+ implementation).
* **Virtual Agent Pricing:** Sold as an enterprise add-on module (quoted per seat/tech or volume tier, typically adding $50–$150/user/mo plus per-minute voice consumption fees).
* **Accessibility:** Completely inaccessible and cost-prohibitive for solo freelancers or 1–3 person trade crews.

## 4. ServiceM8 & Buildertrend
*Source Verified: October 2026 (support.servicem8.com, Authencio, Buildertrend Pricing, Doclio)*

### ServiceM8
* **Target Market:** Small trades, plumbers, electricians, locksmiths (1–20 workers).
* **Pricing Tiers:** Tiered SaaS plans starting from Lite (~$11/mo), Standard ($39/mo), Premium ($79/mo), to Premium+ ($149/mo) for unlimited users.
* **SMS & Texting:** SMS is built-in across all plans using an **SMS credit model** (~$0.08–$0.12 per SMS or prepaid credit packs). Includes two-way SMS conversations routed into the central Job Inbox.
* **AI Features:** **ServiceM8 Phone** (VoIP integration for ~$59/mo flat) includes an AI Call Assistant that records calls, generates automated call transcriptions and summaries, and converts voice notes or phone calls directly into draft Job Cards.

### Buildertrend
* **Target Market:** Home builders, remodelers, commercial specialty contractors.
* **Pricing Tiers:** Essential ($299/mo), Advanced ($499/mo), Complete ($699–$899/mo) flat rate with unlimited users based on active project volume.
* **SMS & Texting:** Client texting and SMS notifications are integrated into the portal/communication suite on Advanced and Complete tiers.
* **AI Features:** **Buildertrend AI** focuses on AI Client Updates (automatically generating polished daily site summaries for clients based on builder field notes) and financial schedule forecasting. Does not offer a standalone AI voice receptionist.

## 6. A2P 10DLC Context & Deliverability
*Source Verified: October 2026 (Twilio A2P Center, TCR, Telgorithm, Reddit r/smallbusiness, Capterra)*

### What is A2P 10DLC?
Under US wireless carrier regulations (AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon), any business sending SMS messages from a 10-digit local number must register their **Brand** (business identity/EIN) and **Campaign** (use case, e.g., customer service/appointment reminders) with The Campaign Registry (TCR).

### Fees & Carrier Charges
* **Brand Registration Fee:** ~$4.40–$44 (one-time).
* **Campaign Submission Fee:** ~$15 (one-time per submission).
* **Monthly Campaign Fee:** ~$1.50–$10/month (recurring).
* **Per-Message Carrier Surcharges:** ~$0.003–$0.005 per SMS segment.

### Jobber & Housecall Pro Handling
Jobber and Housecall Pro facilitate A2P 10DLC registration via in-app forms connected to upstream CPaaS providers (Twilio/Bandwidth). While they attempt to streamline the flow, users are still required to submit EINs, legal addresses, and sample messages. Registration fees are either absorbed as overhead or passed through during setup.

### Customer Complaints & Review Pain Points
A2P 10DLC enforcement is one of the most prominent sources of negative reviews across G2, Capterra, and Reddit for home-service CRMs:
1. **Approval Bottlenecks:** Vetting takes 2 to 4 weeks, leaving new contractors unable to text clients during their onboarding phase.
2. **Campaign Rejections:** High rejection rates caused by websites lacking compliant privacy policies (specifically missing explicit language stating SMS opt-in data will not be shared with third parties) or non-compliant opt-in checkboxes on booking forms.
3. **Undelivered Messages:** Carrier spam filters silently dropping automated texts ("Message failed to deliver") when unverified local numbers are used.
4. **Mandatory Opt-Out Mandates:** Carrier enforcement requiring "Reply STOP to unsubscribe" footers on outbound texts, which contractors complain feels cold and corporate to local residential customers.

### Voice vs. SMS Allowance Packaging Analysis

#### The Problem with Pooled Units
TrueAxis HQ currently defines plans by Voice Minutes (Starter 300, Pro 1000, Agency 2500). **Do NOT merge Voice Minutes and SMS into a single shared pool counter.** 
Wholesale costs for AI voice minutes (~$0.10–$0.20/min including LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and telephony) are roughly **10x higher** than SMS message segments (~$0.008–$0.015/msg including carrier fees). A pooled model risks margin collapse if a user consumes high voice volume or confusion over unit conversion rates.

#### Recommended Dual-Allowance Structure
Establish explicit, separate monthly allowances for **AI Voice Minutes** and **SMS Messages**:

1. **Starter Plan ($49–$69/month):**
   * **300 AI Voice Minutes / mo** (AI receptionist handles ~60–100 calls)
   * **1,000 SMS Messages / mo** (Two-Way SMS conversations & automated reminders)
   * *Target:* Solo freelancers, single-operator trade pros.
2. **Pro Plan ($149–$179/month):**
   * **1,000 AI Voice Minutes / mo** (~200–350 calls)
   * **3,000 SMS Messages / mo** (Two-Way SMS, review requests, drip follow-ups)
   * *Target:* Growing 2–5 person trade crews needing automated dispatching.
3. **Agency / Scale Plan ($299/month):**
   * **2,500 AI Voice Minutes / mo** (~500–800 calls)
   * **7,500 SMS Messages / mo** (Full SMS marketing campaigns & high-volume texting)
   * *Target:* Multi-truck operations and busy trade agencies.
