# TrueAxis HQ — Public Terms Legal-Claim Correction

**Prepared:** August 26, 2026  
**Evidence labels:** Implemented and validated; Copy correction

## Correction

The public Terms introduction previously described the Terms as a universally legally binding agreement. That conclusion was not supported by a jurisdiction-specific legal review, a validated acceptance flow for every use case, or evidence of enforceability in all circumstances.

The public introduction now describes the Terms as platform policies intended to govern access and use and tells the owner to obtain qualified legal review before relying on them for a particular jurisdiction or use case. No substantive terms, platform behavior, or access controls were changed.
