# Owner Clarity Workflow Audit

**Evidence label:** **Research-supported audit; implementation claims are limited to verified TrueAxis HQ workflows.**

HoneyBook’s official workflow guidance emphasizes clearly defined lead nurture, client onboarding, client approval, and offboarding stages, including explicit next steps and documented approvals.[1] TrueAxis HQ already has booking/intake, proposals/contracts, portal next-step guidance, approvals, documents, job templates, proof timelines, automations, and offboarding-related testimonial/review flows. The principal remaining usability risk is not missing a generic category but making those steps discoverable and understandable for a busy owner.

The current evidence-supported owner-clarity direction is therefore to prioritize visible next actions, reusable task checklists, approval status, client-safe files, and a concise command surface. These are implemented in narrow bounded forms. Live workflow completion time, actual owner comprehension, staff coordination quality, and client satisfaction remain external validation questions.
