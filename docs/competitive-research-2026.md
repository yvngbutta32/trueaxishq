# Competitive Research — 2026

## Evidence Summary

The current category baseline is an integrated lead-to-payment workflow: lead capture, forms, proposals/contracts, scheduling, invoicing, online payment, recurring billing, automated reminders, and a client portal. Dubsado presents these as its core suite, including smart-field contracts, workflows, client portals, scheduling, payment reminders, and recurring billing.[1] Jobber extends this operationally for service businesses with request intake, estimates, appointment scheduling, job tracking, client-facing payments, field photos, client communications, and automated review/referral outreach.[2]

The competitive research identifies two consistent pain clusters. First, independent professionals lose work when follow-ups, conversation history, and client information are fragmented across tools.[3] Second, broad platforms frequently become difficult to configure or leave the client portal as a passive document-viewing experience instead of an interactive delivery space.[4]

## Strategic Direction

The product should not copy every competitor feature. TrueAxis HQ’s defensible position should be an **AI-assisted, evidence-first operating system for independent service businesses**: from inquiry to paid work, each client sees a clear, mobile-friendly journey; each owner sees the next best action, the financial consequence, and proof of delivery in one place.

The first candidate investments from this research are: (1) a true client-facing request and message workflow rather than a read-mostly portal; (2) a single job workspace that connects estimate photos, scope, client approvals, work-in-progress evidence, invoices, and payment; (3) delivery and payment automations with measurable conversion/follow-up outcomes; and (4) guided setup that gives a usable system within minutes rather than demanding a complex workflow build.
